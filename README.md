# Morspell

## Morphological species delimitation tool-kit

A repository with standard numerical analyses (statistical or not) used in species delimintation when the only line of evidence is morphology, and, oftentimes, few samples.

## Datasets

1. Squirrels

370 samples, 23 characters (all continuous).

2. Ants

175 samples, 19 characters (all continuous).

3. Corals

74 samples, 27 characters (16 discrete and 11 continuous).

4. Sedges

489 samples, 17 characters (3 discrete and 14 continuous).

5. Hydrocotyle

282 samples, 9 characters (all continuous)

6. Frogs

59 samples, 19 characters (all continuous)
